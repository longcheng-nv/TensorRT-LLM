def indexer_topk_decode(logits, K, N, BS, preIdx):
    routed = (N >= 65536) and (32 <= BS <= 128)              # GVR mid-BS valley
    if not routed:

Why this combination beats sglang_v2: at small N / extreme BS the dispatch uses sglang's OWN skeleton (8-CTA MLP, 4.7-6.7µs launch floor — unbeatable by the GVR 1-CTA skeleton's ~9.7µs floor), paying only the 0.4-0.8% guard tax to buy back unconditional exactness; in the mid-BS valley (N≥65536, BS 32-128) sglang's cluster pool saturates and degrades, while GVR exploits the preIdx temporal prior to get away with 2 full-row reads + a single-pass multi-threshold admission — 1.05-1.57× faster; at large N·K the serial leader P4 becomes the bottleneck (op35 attribution: median 37%), and dist_p4 spreads the histogram + scatter across the whole cluster for a further win up to 1.36×.
