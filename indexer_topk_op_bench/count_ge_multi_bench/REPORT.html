TL;DR速览 — Fusing M thresholds into ONE scan is nearly free at M=2 and cheap at M=4 because block_count_ge is memory-bound: the row is read once and only M cheap predicated compares are added. Evaluating 4 thresholds costs ~1.25–1.40× a single scan instead of 4×, so an M-ary Phase-2 refinement reaches the K-th rank in far fewer total scans. M=4 is the robust sweet spot (small-N latency-bound cells even favor M=6–8; the largest fp16 cell favors M=2).把 M 个阈值融合进一次 scan,在 M=2 几乎免费、M=4 也很便宜——因为 block_count_ge 是 memory-bound:一行只读一次,只多做 M 次廉价谓词比较。评估 4 个阈值仅需 ~1.25–1.40× 单次 scan(而非 4×),故 M-ary 的 Phase-2 细化能以远少的总 scan 数找到第 K 名。M=4 是稳健甜点(小 N 的 latency-bound cell 甚至偏向 M=6–8;最大的 fp16 cell 偏向 M=2)。

5Key findings & recommendation关键结论与建议

Measured实测
  • fp32: mean overhead M2 ×1.02, M4 ×1.25, M6 ×1.46, M8 ×1.77; amortized/threshold at M4 = 0.31.
  • fp16: mean overhead M2 ×1.11, M4 ×1.40, M6 ×1.71, M8 ×2.12; amortized/threshold at M4 = 0.35.
  • fp32:平均倍率 M2 ×1.02、M4 ×1.25、M6 ×1.46、M8 ×1.77;M4 每阈值摊销 = 0.31。
  • fp16:平均倍率 M2 ×1.11、M4 ×1.40、M6 ×1.71、M8 ×2.12;M4 每阈值摊销 = 0.35。
Two regimes两个 regime — Small N (4–8K) is latency-bound: the single CTA stalls on memory, the M compares are fully hidden, so even M=8 is ≤1.2× and the net-benefit favors M=6–8. Large N (128–256K) is bandwidth/compute-exposed: the M compares surface (M=8 up to 2.4× fp32 / 3.4× fp16), pulling the optimum back to M=4 (fp16 256K even to M=2). fp16 steepens earlier because it reads half the bytes → memory finishes sooner → compares dominate at smaller N.小 N(4–8K)为 latency-bound:单 CTA 卡在内存延迟,M 次比较被完全掩盖,故 M=8 也 ≤1.2×,净收益偏向 M=6–8。大 N(128–256K)为 bandwidth/compute-exposed:比较开始显现(M=8 达 fp32 2.4× / fp16 3.4×),把最优拉回 M=4(fp16 256K 甚至到 M=2)。fp16 更早变陡:读的字节减半 → 内存更早完成 → 比较在更小 N 就占主导。
Recommendation建议 — Use M=4 for the GVR Phase-2 refine: near-free at small/mid N and still net-positive at 256K, converging in ~log₅ rounds (~2.3× fewer than binary). Do NOT push M to 6–8 for large N — the added compares/registers erode the gain and you are effectively rebuilding a tiny histogram; hand the final K-th resolution to the existing Phase-4 histogram (all bins in one scan). Design a block_count_ge_multi<M> with M a compile-time constant so the M compares and M-wide reduce fully unroll.在 GVR Phase-2 细化用 M=4:小/中 N 近乎免费,256K 仍净赚,约 log₅ 轮收敛(比二分少 ~2.3×)。大 N 不要上到 6–8——额外比较/寄存器吃掉收益,且相当于在重造一个小直方图;最终第 K 名定位交给已有的 Phase-4 直方图(一遍出所有 bin)。实现 block_count_ge_multi<M> 时 M 用编译期常量,使 M 路比较与 M 宽归约完全展开。
