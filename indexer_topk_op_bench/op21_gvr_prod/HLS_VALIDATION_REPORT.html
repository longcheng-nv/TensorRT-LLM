HLS 宿主原型验证:op22 三场景 bundle 上的定理 1–3

scripts/proto_hls.py · 78 个 fp32 行(best / worst / real × K512/1024/2048 × N 4K–1M)· 验证→优化循环直至族内全局最优 · 2026-07-07
TL;DR. The validate→optimize loop converged in three iterations and returned a policy that is the exhaustive-grid optimum of the whole parameter family, stable under leave-one-scenario-out. Two theory components were corrected by the data: (1) the tracked invariant must be the boundary-local retention ρ\* (sample rank of vK), not the mean hit-rate h̄ — the worst scenario has h̄=0.05 but ρ\*/K≈0.2; (2) the rank-space sample-CCDF bridge of Part II §9 is falsified — log-count regula falsi (value-space, exponential-tail assumption) dominates every alternative: mean 1.00 fallback passes, max 1, 0 failures on all 78 rows at α=0.2. Final model-level result: the op22 tail collapses — best-scenario worst case 149.5 → 27.7 µs (deployable) / 21.6 µs (tracking upper bound); real worst case 83.3 → 27.7 µs. The load-bearing change is the fallback fix (log-falsi + cluster-parallel recount); ρ-aware placement adds the last ~5–7% and makes the system insensitive to estimator error.

The 256-bin P1b histogram is indistinguishable from exact order statistics (identical outcome modes; band med 4048 vs 4066) — re-confirming the op21 QBINS finding at the semantics level. The two observed static-failure classes are exactly "ρ\* outside the static bracket", as Theorem 2 predicts.

Theorem 3 (interior aim point) — CONFIRMED

With log-falsi: α=0 (aim at K) → 1.27 mean passes; α=0.2 → 1.00; α=1.0 (aim at kC) → 2.13. An interior aim point strictly dominates both endpoints; the optimum α ≈ 0.2 sits at m\* = K·(kC/K)0.2 ≈ 1.2–1.6×K, consistent with the legacy kFTarget ≈ 1.5–2×K.

Bridge interpolant (Part II §9 / Part I §4d) — FALSIFIED

Forced-fallback on all 78 rows: bisect 1.77 mean / max 6 / 0 fails; bridge 2.06–3.96 mean, 5–18 failures; log-falsi 1.00 mean / max 1 / 0 fails. The "misses are free boundary-density samples" idea does not survive the h→1 stress geometry: there the sample has no support in the bracket, so any rank-space interpolant is unidentified. The exponential-tail (log-count) interpolant uses only measured counts and wins everywhere.

Theorem 1 (regime split M×R) — DIRECTIONALLY CONFIRMED, REVISED

Regime split direction confirmed: fixing placement+fallback buys 1.13× in regime W but 1.30× in regime C, and 2.4× vs the current msc leader-recount (best scenario). Revision: with a 95% fast rate and a 1-pass fallback, the optimizer picks M=3 in BOTH regimes (τ(3)=1.2 beats M4's marginal coverage). The original "M\*=4 in regime C" assumed expensive misses; once misses cost one cluster-parallel pass, width tax dominates. E[T] = τ(M)·P/C + Pr(miss)·1·(P/C + s) is minimized at smaller M — the theorem's own formula, new constants.

5Caveats and the kernel-validation obligation告诫与内核验证义务

  1. All µs are cost-model outputs, not nsys measurements. Pass counts and outcome modes are host-exact; prices are the Part-II constants. The historical red line stands: host projections have collapsed on contact with silicon before (op16: −40..−60% projected → 0.72–0.87× measured). Kernel A/B on the nsys cold-L2 canonical axis + the full exactness gates is mandatory before any adoption claim.
  2. 78 rows, one row per cell; the two stress scenarios are synthetic constructions. Statistical power for "multi-σ" claims is limited; the real-scenario column is the production-relevant one.
  3. est_r0 is a stationarity proxy for cross-step tracking (the estimate a previous step's counts would hand over), not a replayed multi-step chain. A chained validation (temporal-synth multi-step mode, lag-1 ρ) is the natural next experiment; MTP (next_n>1) row-adjacency is untested.
  4. Collect capacity is a scalar model (4096) for a per-thread-slot mechanism; the kernel's true overflow boundary is shape-dependent.
  5. Cluster-parallel fallback recount requires a real DSMEM merge implementation in gvr_msc_op.py (the current leader-recount exists precisely because the vendored P3 prefix contract made it the easy exact choice — iter2's 646/1024-hole lesson).
  1. 所有 µs 都是代价模型输出,不是 nsys 实测。遍数与判定模式是宿主精确的;价格是 Part II 常数。 历史红线仍然有效:host 投影曾在硅上塌过(op16:投影 −40..−60% → 实测 0.72–0.87×)。任何采纳结论之前,必须过 nsys 冷 L2 canonical 轴的内核 A/B + 全套 exactness 门。
  2. 78 行、每 cell 一行;两个压力场景是合成构造。"多 σ"论断的统计功效有限;real 列才是生产相关的。
  3. est_r0 是跨步跟踪的平稳性代理(上一步 counts 会交出的估计),不是重放的多步链。链式验证 (temporal-synth 多步模式,lag-1 ρ)是自然的下一个实验;MTP(next_n>1)行相邻性未测。
  4. collect 容量是标量模型(4096),真实溢出边界依赖 per-thread slot 形状。
  5. cluster 并行 fallback 重数需要在 gvr_msc_op.py 里实现真正的 DSMEM 合并(现在的 leader 重数正是 因为 vendored P3 前缀契约使它成为容易的精确选择 —— iter2 的 646/1024 空洞教训)。
