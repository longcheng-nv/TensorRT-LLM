iter 3 — dist-P1 falsified; C8 tier lands K2048-only

Falsified
dist-P1 (each CTA gathers only K/C preIdx, DSMEM merges rebuild the seeds): event A/B +0.6–1.7µs worse on every K512/K1024 cell. P4 仍是最大单一相位方法(全域 15–33%),但经 iter5/6/11 三轮后其绝对值(2–3.8µs)已贴近 warp 排名 + K 次输出写的物理下限,继续压缩空间有限。K2048 非 fuse 路径的真实大头是 classic collect(31–35%),不是 P4。
Bottleneck ① smallN
Below 32K the bottleneck is phase-chain fixed cost, not memory: at N4K, P4+P3+P1b+front-end fixed ≈ 89%, scan only ~11%. P4 alone (3.2–3.4µs) ≈ half of radix's ENTIRE kernel at that point (≈6.3µs) — the whole reason smallN loses to radix, quantitatively matching the op12 floor analysis.
Bottleneck ② N=32K gap
The dispatch boundary (R-C's N≥65536) strands 32K on single-CTA: scan share rises to 32–34% there, and B300 nsys shows 32K is SLOWER than 65K (K1024 14.29 vs 13.99; K2048 19.28 vs 15.35). C4 below 65K was never tested — the #1 quick-win candidate exposed by this breakdown.
Bottleneck ③ cluster floor
At ≥65K the bottleneck is the ~8–9µs fixed floor (replicated P1 gather×C + P1b + DSMEM merge + cluster barriers + multi-CTA launch tail): 64–69% at 65K, still 43–48% at 262K after scan dilution. This is the quantitative body of the campaign's "scan floor sits at the rival bar"; dist-P1 is falsified (barriers cost more), so it is near-structural on this architecture.
Bottleneck ④ P4 status
P4 remains the largest single phase method (15–33% everywhere), but after iter5/6/11 its absolute cost (2–3.8µs) sits near the physical floor of warp ranking + K output writes — little headroom left. On the K2048 unfused path the real top item is classic collect (31–35%), not P4.
