4 · Step 3 — S1b ρ̂-EMA 自适应放置：被 S1a 支配，数据关闭4 · Step 3 — S1b ρ̂-EMA adaptive placement: dominated by S1a, closed with data

在 29,820 个真实 Pro 转移上、以 S1a 宽梯为新基线重放：EMA 对称带快路 0.851、低侧非对称带 0.871，均低于静态 0.958（模型 E[T] 慢 0.2-1.1%）。宽静态梯已吸收 EMA 能买到的 h 信息，而 σ̂≈0.1 的过程方差地板让 h-居中窗漏得更多。到 oracle 的残差 ~4pts 没有因果 tracker 能实现。永久关闭，不建 kernel、不加侧张量（替代 iter15.3 的“diet 后重估”悬案：重估 = 否决）。

Replayed on 29,820 real Pro transitions ON TOP of the S1a wide ladder: EMA symmetric band 0.851 fast, low-side-asymmetric 0.871 — both below the static 0.958 (0.2-1.1% slower model E[T]). The ~4pt residual to oracle is unreachable by any causal tracker. Permanently closed — no kernel, no side tensor (resolves iter15.3's "re-evaluate after the diet": re-evaluation = rejection).

6 · Step 5 — 小 N：kC 减肥 wash；地板三角定位为结构性6 · Step 5 — small N: kC diet wash; the floor triangulated as structural

Host screen: kC/2 is admission-free at N≤32K. Silicon (16 real cells): ship/kc50 gm 0.994 = wash. op21's rank-scatter P4 is barrier-bound, not cand-bound (closes the loop with op12; op13's 10-15% win was secant-snap-specific). S3b warp pipelining not re-run a 4th time: op8 NCU (structural occupancy at BS=1, pipeline ≤4%) + op15 warm-L2 (phase-chain latency, not memory tier) + this kc50 wash (work-reduction insensitivity) triangulate the same floor.
