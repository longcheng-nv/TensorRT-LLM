## Notes

- **Iter 1 (KEPT):** `harness.py` implements the typed API, the FC1(SwiGLU)/FC2 source-tagged twin,
  metrics (measured/predicted/rho/Higham μ_F/budget/cosine), and ρ-gated `cross_term` escalation.
  Run: `python3 harness.py` → `results/iteration1.json`.
- **Correction surfaced by the loop:** the FC1 `flip_risk` metric was first mis-defined (counted
  already-saturated elements via a signed clamp margin → spurious 0.605). Fixed to distance-to-
  threshold vs perturbation (boundary-*crossing*), now 0.14. The harness owns the number, so the
  bug was caught before any claim was made on it.
- This single pass also effectively closed the host portions of **GA3 (fake-quant nvf4/mxf8/mxf4
  + TF32-off fp64 ref), GA4 (unified metrics), GA6 (cross-term)** — they are exercised by iter 1.
